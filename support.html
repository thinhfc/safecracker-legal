How to play

Tap anywhere when the gold needle crosses the cyan pin. Each hit reverses the needle and speeds it up. Miss and the alarm goes off. Crack every pin to open the vault. You can replay the tutorial any time from Home → How to play.

Restore your Pro purchase

Open Settings → Restore purchases (or the Restore button on the Pro screen). Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID used to buy Pro.

No sound or vibration?

Check Settings → Sound effects / Haptics in the app. Sound follows the Ring/Silent switch on your iPhone.
