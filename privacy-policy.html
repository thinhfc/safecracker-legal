SafeCracker: Haptic Lock ASMR — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026

Summary

SafeCracker does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal data. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It works fully offline.

Data stored on your device

The app saves your game progress and preferences (level, best combo, coins, selected mode, appearance, language, sound and haptics settings, and whether you own Pro) only on your device, using standard iOS storage. This data never leaves your device through us. Deleting the app deletes it.

Purchases

The optional one-time purchase “Pro” is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, email, payment details or Apple ID. Apple’s handling of purchase data is described in Apple’s privacy policy.

Tracking and third parties

  • No third-party SDKs, advertising or analytics.
  • No tracking across apps or websites; the app never asks for tracking permission.
  • The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, photos, contacts or location.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
